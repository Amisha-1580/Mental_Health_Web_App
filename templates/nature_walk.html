5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Walk

As you walk, tune your attention to the world around you:

5
Notice 5 things you can SEE

Tree bark texture, dancing shadows, cloud shapes, colors.

4
Notice 4 things you can TOUCH

Cool air on your cheeks, feet pressing the ground, a smooth stone.

3
Notice 3 things you can HEAR

Bird calls, rustling leaves, distant water, your steady breath.

2
Notice 2 things you can SMELL

Fresh pine, damp earth, blooming flora, crisp fresh rain.

1
Notice 1 thing you FEEL GRATITUDE for

The ability to move your body freely and breathe fresh air.
