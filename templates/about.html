About Mood Map

Empowering individuals through situational awareness and empathetic support.

Mood Map is designed to grade the mental health state of a person and suggest tips to improve and maintain it. Our featured AI chatbot provides a safe space for users to interact and express themselves.

It is a way of knowing the User's mood and mental health state and improve it without directly asking them by situational questions and their behaviour throughout the application journey.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, personalized mental health insights using modern AI technology and situational analysis.

Our Vision

A world where everyone has the tools to understand their mental well-being and take proactive steps towards health.
