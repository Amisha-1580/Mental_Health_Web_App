The 3 Good Things Practice

Developed by positive psychology researchers (Dr. Martin Seligman), writing down three positive events each day helps counteract the brain's natural negativity bias.

💡 Studies show doing this for just 7 consecutive days significantly increases happiness and lowers depressive symptoms for up to 6 months.
